## 💡 The Vision

Creating high-performing Instagram content is demanding. Creators lose hours each week suffering from **caption writer’s block**, guessing **trending audio**, researching **effective hashtags**, debating between **dozens of similar photos**, and struggling to maintain **consistent community engagement**.

**InstaGenie** replaces that friction with an intuitive, multimodal AI workspace. Powered by **Google Gemini Vision**, InstaGenie analyzes your images and creative intent to deliver platform-native, authentic, high-converting content in seconds—not hours.

> *"InstaGenie's got your back. Get badass AI-powered captions, hashtags, and music to make your posts look premium."*

### 1. 📸 Post Studio — High-Converting Static & Carousel Posts
Transform everyday photos into high-engagement feed posts with contextual AI intelligence.

- **Multimodal AI Captions:** Upload an image or provide a prompt. InstaGenie analyzes the visual atmosphere, scene, colors, and subject to draft natural, engaging, human-sounding captions (no generic "living my best life" fluff).
- **Tone Customization:** Switch seamlessly across **15+ distinct voices** — *Aesthetic, Confident, Chill, Funny, Flirty, Sarcastic, Polite, or Professional*.
- **Smart Hashtag Generator:** Replaces hashtag spam with strategic, high-relevance tags categorized by reach and niche competition.
- **Audio & Soundtrack Matcher:** Computer-vision analysis identifies the vibe, setting, and energy of your image to suggest authentic, trending real-world audio tracks that elevate your aesthetic.

---

### 2. 🎬 Reel Studio — Viral Short-Form Video Engine
Engineered to capture viewer attention in the critical first 3 seconds.

- **Trending Topic Discovery:** Generates high-velocity content concepts and viral hooks aligned with your specific niche.
- **Script & Description Architect:** Crafts hook-driven descriptions that tease value, suspense, or emotion before viewers scroll past.
- **Reel-Optimized Micro-Captions:** Punchy, fast-reading text designed specifically for short-form video consumer habits.
- **Audio Sync & Story Beats:** Recommends trending sound bites and music tracks tailored to the tempo and mood of your video.

---

### 3. ⚡ Story Studio — Ephemeral, High-Retention Moments
Keep your audience coming back every 24 hours with interactive, snappy story content.

- **Quick Story Hooks:** Conversational captions crafted for fast taps and immediate direct-message replies.
- **Contextual Emoji & Sticker Recommender:** Suggests the ideal emoji palettes and interactive elements (polls, question stickers, sliders) to drive story taps.
- **Mood Beats:** Ambient, chill, or upbeat background sound pairings tailored to daily lifestyle snippets.

---

### 4. 🖼️ AI Photo Picker — Visual Curation & Selection Engine
Overcome decision paralysis when choosing between dozens of similar shots from a photoshoot.

- **Comparative Multi-Image Analysis:** Upload 2 to 5 photos from a single session.
- **Custom Evaluation Priorities:** Rank images based on creator-defined criteria:
  - *Face Clarity & Sharpness (no blur)*
  - *Smile & Natural Candid Expression*
  - *Aesthetic & Color Palette Vibes*
  - *Lighting & Background Composition*
  - *Confidence & Body Language*
- **Explainable AI Decisioning:** Delivers the #1 best photo and top 3 ranked choices accompanied by transparent, actionable rationale explaining *why* it will outperform the others.

---

### 5. 💬 Engagement & Reply Hub — Turn Lurkers into Loyal Followers
Instagram rewards active community interaction. InstaGenie turns engagement into a 1-click breeze.

- **Post & Story Commentator:** Feed an image or context into the engine to receive natural, perceptive comments that spark conversations on other creators' profiles.
- **Smart Reply Assistant:** Paste incoming follower comments or DMs to generate diverse replies spanning *Appreciative, Witty, Flirty, Roast, Chill, or Respectful* tones.
- **One-Click Clipboard Workflow:** Fast copying designed for quick multitasking across your mobile and desktop apps.

---

### 6. 🎨 Creator Dashboard & Interactive Experience
A bespoke creator workspace built with performance and design flair.

- **Immersive 3D Graphics:** Fluid interactive 3D hero canvas powered by Three.js and React Three Fiber.
- **Custom DiceBear Avatars:** Dynamic avatar generation across multiple styles (*Avataaars, Bottts, Croodles*).
- **Service Usage Telemetry:** Transparent, real-time counters monitoring captions generated, audio suggestions received, hashtags researched, and photos evaluated.
- **Theme-Adaptive UI:** Seamless dark and light mode aesthetics built with Tailwind CSS.

## 🛠️ Architecture & Tech Stack

```
[ Client Browser ]
       │
       ▼ Next.js 15 (App Router + React 19 + Tailwind v4 + Three.js)
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   Next.js Server API                   │
│  ├── /api/generate-caption     ├── /api/photo-picker   │
│  ├── /api/generate-hashtag     ├── /api/reels/*        │
│  ├── /api/generate-audio       ├── /api/stories/*      │
│  └── /api/generate-comments    └── /api/auth & credits │
└──────────────┬─────────────────────────┬───────────────┘
               │                         │
               ▼                         ▼
      [ Google Gemini AI ]        [ MongoDB Atlas ]
   • gemini-2.5-flash-lite        • User Credentials & Profiles
   • gemini-2.5-flash (Multimodal)• Service Telemetry & Usage Logs
   • Vision & Prompt Reasoning    • Plan & Expiry Management
```

- **Frontend:** Next.js 15, React 19, Tailwind CSS v4, Lucide React, Three.js, `@react-three/fiber`, `@react-three/drei`.
- **AI Intelligence:** Google Generative AI SDK (`@google/generative-ai`) utilizing `gemini-2.5-flash` and `gemini-2.5-flash-lite` with structured prompt engineering and vision encoding.
- **Avatars & Identity:** DiceBear (`@dicebear/core`, `@dicebear/collection`).
- **Database & Persistence:** MongoDB with Mongoose ODM for atomic credit tracking and usage stats.
- **Security & Authentication:** Custom JWT (`jose`, `jsonwebtoken`), bcrypt password hashing, and Next.js middleware session protection.
- **Payments:** Razorpay payment flow with plan auto-provisioning.

## ⚡ Quick Start (For Developers)

To run InstaGenie locally on your machine:

### 1. Prerequisites
- Node.js 20+
- MongoDB instance (local or Atlas)
- Google Gemini API Key ([Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/))

### 2. Setup Environment
Clone the repository and create your `.env` file:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/your-username/instagenie.git
cd instagenie
```

Create a `.env` file in the root:
```env
GEMINI_API_KEY=your_gemini_api_key
MONGODB_URI=your_mongodb_connection_string
JWT_SECRET=your_secure_jwt_secret
```

### 3. Install & Run
```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

Visit [`http://localhost:3000`](http://localhost:3000) to explore the app.
